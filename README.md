## 🔍 Understanding the Technology

This software combines several methods to fix images. CodeFormer acts as a reconstruction tool. It looks at a face in a blurry image and compares it to thousands of clear faces it has learned previously. It then rebuilds the features of the face to match high-quality standards.

GFPGAN serves as a secondary layer. It focuses on the structure of the face. It ensures that the eyes, nose, and mouth appear in the correct positions even if the original photo suffered from heavy blur.

The enhancement process involves several stages:

1. **Detection:** The software finds faces within your uploaded file.
2. **Analysis:** The AI examines pixels to determine which parts are blurry and which parts are clear.
3. **Restoration:** The models replace the blurry pixels with realistic, sharp data.
4. **Blending:** The software merges the restored face with the original background of your photograph.
